# Contact — say what broke

One address, and it reaches the person who wrote the page.

**contact@clipunder.com**

## What is worth writing about

- A file that will not open, or that opens and then stops halfway. Say which container and
  codec it was — `ffprobe` output if you have it.
- An encode that came out visibly worse than it should have, or well under the target size
  when it did not need to be.
- A limit on this site that no longer matches what the platform actually allows.

## What to expect

Best effort, usually within a few days. This is a one-person project, not a support desk,
and there is no account to look into — your files never reached this site, so there is
nothing on this end to inspect.

## Privacy of your message

Your message and address are used to answer you and for nothing else. There is no
newsletter.
