# Privacy — your video stays on your device

This page describes what the site actually does. Last updated 30 October 2026.

## Your files

Your video is never uploaded. It is read from your disk by your browser, decoded,
re-encoded and written back as a download — all inside the tab. There is no server in that
path, so there is nothing that could store, copy or share your file. Close the tab and the
work is gone.

## What is collected

The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics script. There is no account system,
so there is no profile of you to build.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the request
that delivers this page — IP address, browser user agent, the file requested — in order to
serve it and to block abuse. That is standard web server logging.

## Consent

Advertising and analytics storage is off by default. Nothing that needs consent is loaded
before you give it, and the choice is stored in your browser rather than on a server.

## Email

If you write to contact@clipunder.com, your message and address are kept only to answer
you. There is no newsletter and no list to be added to.
